How to record card activity

One ledger entry keeps balances, history and reporting consistent.

01
Card purchase

Add an Expense, choose the credit-card account, and select the appropriate expense category. The amount owed increases automatically.

02
Pay a credit card

Add a Transfer from your bank or cash account to the credit-card account. Choose CC Payment. The card amount owed decreases automatically.

03
Move money to investments

Use a Transfer between accounts and choose Investment. For ordinary movements between your own accounts, choose Self-Transfer.
